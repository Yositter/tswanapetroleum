    From loading gantry to your tank

    Fuel goes missing in the gaps between handovers. We close each gap with a document you can check against your own records.

    1. 1

      Load

      Loaded at a licensed terminal. Volume is measured and corrected to 20 °C.

      You receiveLoading certificate

    2. 2

      Seal

      Every compartment is sealed at the gantry and each seal number is logged.

      You receiveSeal register

    3. 3

      Track

      The control room follows each truck by GPS and flags any unplanned stop.

      You receiveTrip report with GPS trace

    4. 4

      Deliver

      Your tank is dipped before and after. Product is metered in with your team present.

      You receiveSigned, metered delivery ticket

    5. 5

      Reconcile

      Every delivery is matched against your stock and your invoices each month.

      You receiveMonthly reconciliation statement

    The control room is staffed 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Call it for emergency supply or to trace any load in transit.

    How your price is built

    South African fuel prices follow a regulated framework that adjusts on the first Wednesday of each month. Three parts of your price sit outside our control. The other two are ours, and we fix them in your supply agreement.

    Components of a delivered fuel price
    ComponentWhat drives itControlled byWhen it changes
    Basic fuel priceInternational product prices, freight and the rand–dollar exchange rateInternational marketsMonthly
    Fuel and RAF leviesGeneral fuel levy and Road Accident Fund levyNational TreasuryWith the national Budget, usually in April
    Inland transportDistance from the coast to your pricing zoneRegulated zone differentialsPeriodically, by regulation
    Wholesale marginOur cost of supply and serviceTswana PetroleumFixed for your agreement term
    DeliveryDrop size, distance and site accessTswana PetroleumQuoted upfront per site
