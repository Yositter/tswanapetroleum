Accounts

Apply for a trade credit account

Open a fuel supply account with agreed payment terms. Complete the application below. A credit manager reviews every application and replies within two business days. Approval is subject to credit assessment and our standard terms of supply.

Before you begin

Have your company registration, VAT number and banking details to hand. You will also need two trade references. Providing complete information speeds up assessment.

Approval depends on a credit assessment, which may include a credit bureau enquiry and reference checks. We assess each application on its own merits.

Prefer paperwork? Request the printable credit application and standard supply agreement from accounts@tswanapetroleum.co.za.
