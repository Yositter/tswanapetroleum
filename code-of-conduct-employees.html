Governance

Employee Code of Conduct

This code sets the standard of behaviour expected of every person who works for Tswana Petroleum, whether in the control room, at the gantry, behind the wheel or in the office. It is a condition of employment and applies without exception.

Purpose and scope

We handle dangerous goods, hold customer funds and data, and carry the company's name onto every site we serve. Trust is our licence to operate. This code translates that trust into clear expectations. It applies to all employees, directors, contractors and temporary staff, in every country where we work.

Where this code and local law differ, the higher standard applies. Where a situation is not covered here, apply the principle behind the code and ask your manager before acting.

The standard

  1. Put safety first, always

    Follow every safety and dangerous-goods procedure without shortcuts. Do not drive while impaired or fatigued. Stop any task that cannot be done safely and report the hazard. No delivery, deadline or instruction justifies putting a person at risk.

  2. Protect every litre and every record

    Seal, meter and document product exactly as procedure requires. Never adulterate, divert, or misrepresent volumes or quality. The integrity of the delivery ticket, seal register and reconciliation is the integrity of the company.

  3. Refuse bribery and corruption

    Do not offer, give, solicit or accept any bribe, kickback or improper payment, in any form, to anyone, including public officials. Gifts and hospitality must be modest, transparent and never given to influence a decision. When in doubt, decline and disclose.

  4. Avoid conflicts of interest

    Declare any personal, family or financial interest that could influence your work, including outside business, related suppliers and customers. Do not use your position, company assets or information for private gain.

  5. Guard information and personal data

    Protect confidential company, customer and colleague information. Handle personal information lawfully under POPIA: collect only what is needed, use it only for its purpose, and keep it secure. Do not share credentials or customer data outside approved channels.

  6. Treat people with dignity

    We do not tolerate discrimination, harassment, bullying or victimisation of any kind. Everyone is entitled to a safe and respectful workplace. We support fair employment, and we do not use child or forced labour anywhere in our operations.

  7. Deal fairly in the market

    Compete on merit. Do not fix prices, rig bids or share commercially sensitive information with competitors. Represent our products and services honestly to customers.

  8. Respect the environment and communities

    Prevent spills, manage waste responsibly, and minimise the footprint of your work. Respect the communities and landowners along our corridors and on our sites.

  9. Keep honest records

    Record transactions, times, volumes and expenses accurately and never falsify a document. Cooperate fully and truthfully with audits and investigations.

Speaking up

A concern raised early prevents harm. Raising it is protected.

If you see or suspect a breach of this code, a safety risk, or unlawful conduct, report it. Speak to your manager, or use the confidential channel below if that is not appropriate. You may report anonymously.

We investigate every genuine report fairly and act on the findings. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious breach of this code and will be treated as such. Breaches may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and, where the law requires, referral to the authorities.

Confidential conduct and safety line: conduct@tswanapetroleum.co.za. Reports may be made anonymously.
