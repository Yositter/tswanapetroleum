Supplier Code of Conduct

Every transporter, vendor and partner who works with Tswana Petroleum carries our name and our risk. This code sets the standards we require of them. Accepting a purchase order or supply agreement is acceptance of this code.

Who this applies to

This code applies to all suppliers of goods and services, transporters and subcontractors, and their own employees and subcontractors. We expect our suppliers to hold their supply chains to equivalent standards and to be able to demonstrate that they do.

We assess suppliers against this code at onboarding and during the relationship. We prefer suppliers who share our commitment to safety, empowerment and honest trade, and we support local and B-BBEE aligned enterprises to meet these standards rather than simply screening them out.

What we require

  1. Safety and dangerous-goods compliance

    Maintain roadworthy, compliant vehicles and equipment. Employ trained, licensed and medically fit drivers and operators. Follow all dangerous-goods, loading and site-safety requirements. Report incidents involving our product or name without delay.

  2. Lawful operation and licensing

    Hold and maintain all licences, permits and insurances required for your work, including transport, environmental and petroleum-handling authorisations. Comply with all applicable laws in every country of operation.

  3. Anti-bribery and fair dealing

    Do not offer or accept bribes, kickbacks or facilitation payments in connection with our business. Do not collude on pricing or bids. Declare any conflict of interest, including any relationship with our employees.

  4. Labour and human rights

    No child labour and no forced or bonded labour, anywhere in your operations or supply chain. Pay at least the legal minimum wage, respect working-time laws, and provide a safe workplace free of discrimination and harassment.

  5. Environmental responsibility

    Prevent spills and pollution, manage waste lawfully, and work to reduce the environmental footprint of the services you provide to us. Carry the means to contain and report a product spill.

  6. Protect information and data

    Keep our commercial information and any personal data confidential and secure, use it only for the agreed purpose, and handle personal information in line with POPIA.

  7. Honest records and cooperation

    Invoice accurately for goods and services actually delivered. Keep truthful records. Allow reasonable audit and verification of compliance with this code, and cooperate with any investigation.

  8. Empowerment and local development

    Support the growth of local and black-owned enterprise where you can, including subcontracting, skills transfer and fair payment terms to smaller suppliers.

Compliance and reporting

The code is a term of doing business with us.

We may verify compliance through documentation, site visits or audit. A supplier who breaches this code will be required to correct the breach within an agreed period. Serious or repeated breaches, and any involvement in bribery, fraud, or labour or safety abuses, may lead to suspension or termination of the relationship and, where the law requires, referral to the authorities.

If you become aware of any breach of this code involving our business, or have a concern about the conduct of our own people, report it in confidence.

Supplier conduct line: conduct@tswanapetroleum.co.za. To become an approved supplier, request the onboarding pack from the procurement section.
