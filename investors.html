Investors

An independent supplier built on accountability

Tswana Petroleum is an independent, empowerment-focused fuel distributor serving mining, agriculture, construction, transport, aviation and industry across the SADC region. Our investment case rests on disciplined operations, transparent governance and a defensible position in an essential market.

The investment case

Essential demand

Fuel underpins mining, agriculture, logistics and power across the region. Demand is durable and diversified across sectors and geographies.

Operational moat

Sealed, metered, reconciled custody and a 24-hour control room turn reliability and loss control into a service customers will not easily switch away from.

Corridor reach

Seven supply corridors from seven ports give redundancy and access that a single-port supplier cannot match.

Regulated, transparent pricing

Most of the delivered price follows a published regulatory framework. Our margin and delivery components are fixed and visible, which supports predictable relationships.

Empowerment credentials

A B-BBEE aligned, empowerment-focused business well positioned for public-sector and listed-company procurement.

Disciplined governance

Codes of conduct, POPIA and PAIA compliance, and framework-aligned sustainability reporting reduce risk for financiers and partners.

Governance and reporting

We report on more than earnings.

We are committed to transparent governance and to sustainability reporting aligned with recognised standards. Our intention is to disclose environmental, social and governance performance alongside financial results, in a form suited to South African financiers and to listed customers who must account for their own supply chains.

  • Annual and interim financial results, once published
  • Integrated and sustainability reporting aligned to recognised frameworks
  • Responsibility programme and commitments
  • Employee and supplier codes of conduct
  • Company registration and petroleum wholesale licence details, available on request

Investor relations: investors@tswanapetroleum.co.za. Reporting documents and the reporting calendar are published here as they become available.
