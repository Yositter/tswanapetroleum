Leadership

A message from the Chairman

On accountability, empowerment and the discipline of moving fuel safely across a region that depends on it.

To our customers, partners and people

Fuel is the quiet input behind almost everything the Southern African economy does. It turns the drill and the harvester, moves the container and the patient, and keeps the lights on when the grid does not. A business that supplies it holds a position of trust. At Tswana Petroleum we have built the company around a single idea that our people repeat every day: every litre, accounted for.

That phrase is not marketing. It is an operating discipline. It means product loaded at a licensed terminal, sealed at the gantry, tracked in transit, metered into your tank and reconciled against your records. It means a customer pays for exactly what they receive, and a community is not exposed to fuel that has been diverted or adulterated. In a market where losses in the gaps between handovers are common, we have chosen to close every gap with a document you can check.

This year the region tested that discipline. Currency movements and international product prices pushed costs up and down within the regulated framework, and logistics along the corridors remained demanding. Through it we held our delivery reliability and kept our control room staffed every hour of every day. I am most proud, however, of the record that matters most: our commitment to the safety of our people and the public on the roads we travel.

Responsibility runs wider than safety. We are an independent, empowerment-focused business, and we take seriously our role in developing local suppliers and transporters, training drivers, and creating a path into skilled work for young people and women in an industry that has too few of them. Our codes of conduct, for our own people and for our suppliers, set a plain standard: trade honestly, refuse corruption, and treat people with dignity. We would rather lose business than compromise on that.

The road ahead is one of measured growth. We will deepen our corridor network, invest in cleaner fuels and a lower-carbon operation, and continue to earn the trust of the mines, farms, fleets and industries that rely on us. To our customers, thank you for holding us to a high standard. To our partners and our people, thank you for meeting it.
